# Playbook: Brand New Account (0 Followers)

## Core Principle
A brand-new account has zero trust. The first 30 days are about generating clean, high-quality engagement signals and avoiding anything that looks automated or spammy.

## Phase 1: Days 1–7 (Foundation)
- 45–60 minutes of pure replies every day
- Target 25–40 thoughtful replies in your chosen niche
- Post at most 1 very strong original post (or none)
- Follow only people you actually engage with
- Reply to every notification immediately

**Goal**: Look like a real, active human who adds value.

## Phase 2: Days 8–21
- Continue heavy reply volume (20–30/day)
- Introduce 1 original post per day
- Best first posts: strong opinion + question, useful breakdown, or valuable quote post
- Reply to 100% of comments on your content
- Start forming mutual follows

## Phase 3: Days 22–45
- Maintain 15–25 quality replies daily
- Post 1–2 times per day
- Double down on topics that generate replies and quotes
- Protect against author diversity decay (never flood)

## Universal Rules for New Accounts
- Never buy followers or engagement
- Never use repeated reply templates
- Never ignore people who engage with you
- Never post more than 2–3 times per day in the first 60 days
- Consistency beats intensity

**30-Day Target**: 150–400 followers + a small group of regular engagers if executed well.
