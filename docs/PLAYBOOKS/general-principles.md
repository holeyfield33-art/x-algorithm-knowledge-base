# General Algorithm-Aligned Principles

These rules apply to almost every account.

## Maximize
- Replies (especially mutual-follow original posts)
- Quotes
- Copy-link shares and DM shares
- Follows of the author
- Meaningful conversation loops

## Minimize / Avoid
- Content that triggers reports, mutes, “Not interested”, or blocks
- Posting many times in a short window from the same author (diversity decay)
- Pure like-bait with no reply potential
- Anything that looks inauthentic or automated

## Structural Rules
- Keep most posts under 48 hours old for best eligibility
- Space posts out
- Reply quickly and thoughtfully to people who engage with you
- Build real mutual follows in your niche

## Measurement Focus
Track replies, quotes, shares, and follows generated more closely than raw likes or impressions. These are the signals the ranking model is primarily optimized for.
