# Playbook: AI Security Account (Small / ~15 Followers)

**Target**: Accounts like @1Aihub focused on AI security / agent security / LLM vulnerabilities.

## Core Strategy
At very low follower counts the algorithm has almost no history on you. Growth comes from:
1. High-quality replies in existing AI/security conversations
2. Creating a few strong engagement loops
3. Avoiding any spammy or low-value signals

**Primary Goal**: 15 → 300–500 followers + consistent daily views in 45–60 days.

## Daily System

### Session 1 – Engagement (30–45 min)
- Reply to 20–30 posts in AI security, prompt injection, AI red teaming, model vulnerabilities, agent security, adversarial ML, etc.
- Prefer accounts in the 500–15k follower range.
- Make replies specific and useful (add a technical point, highlight a blind spot, or ask a sharp question).

### Session 2 – Posting (1–2 times max per day)
Best formats (in order):
1. Short sharp take + clear question
2. Breakdown of a real vulnerability or incident
3. Practical checklist / framework
4. Quote post of a bigger account with real technical commentary

**Hard rules**:
- Never more than 2 posts per day
- Space posts at least 4–6 hours apart (diversity decay)
- Reply to every real engagement on your posts

## Weekly Focus
- Mon–Wed: Heavy replies + 1 strong original post/day
- Thu–Fri: One higher-effort piece (thread or detailed breakdown)
- Weekend: Maintain replies, lighter posting

## 45-Day Targets
| Period | Focus | Success Metric |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Days 1–15 | Pure engagement + visibility | 50–80 followers |
| Days 16–30 | Own conversations | 120–200 followers |
| Days 31–45 | Consistent original content | 300+ followers |

## Highest Leverage Actions
1. Become known for thoughtful replies in AI security threads
2. Turn good replies into original posts
3. Build 10–15 mutual follows with other people in the space (unlocks the large reply boost)
